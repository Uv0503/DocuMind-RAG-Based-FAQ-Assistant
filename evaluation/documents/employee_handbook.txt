Northstar Labs Employee Handbook

Working hours and location
The standard work week is forty hours, normally completed from Monday through Friday. Employees may work remotely for up to three days each week after agreeing on a schedule with their manager. New employees should complete their first two weeks from the office so that equipment setup and introductions can be completed.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave during each calendar year. Leave requests longer than five consecutive working days should be submitted at least four weeks in advance. Unused annual leave does not automatically carry into the next year unless local law requires it.

Sick leave
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required when an absence continues for more than three consecutive working days. Sick leave is separate from annual leave and cannot be exchanged for cash.

Learning budget
Every full-time employee has an annual professional-development budget of $1,200. The budget may be used for relevant courses, books, certifications, and conferences after manager approval. It cannot be used to buy general-purpose computer hardware.

Performance reviews
Formal performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. The review discusses results, collaboration, learning goals, and priorities for the following six months. Managers and employees are both expected to provide written input before the meeting.
