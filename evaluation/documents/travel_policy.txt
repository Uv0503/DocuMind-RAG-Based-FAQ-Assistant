Northstar Labs Business Travel Policy

Booking travel
Employees should book approved business travel through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy may be booked for a flight lasting six hours or longer when the department head approves it before purchase.

Hotels
The standard hotel limit is $180 per night before taxes. A higher rate is allowed when the conference hotel is required or when no safe, practical option is available within the limit. The reason for exceeding the limit must be included with the expense report.

Meals
Travelers may claim meal expenses up to $65 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable, and itemized receipts are required for any individual expense of $25 or more. Meals already provided by a hotel, conference, or client should not be claimed again.

Ground transportation
Public transportation should be used when it is safe and practical. Taxis and ride-sharing services are reimbursable for airport transfers, late-night travel, or locations that are not reasonably served by public transit. Car rentals require advance manager approval.

Expense reports
An expense report must be submitted within 15 calendar days after the traveler returns. The report must include the business purpose, dates, destinations, itemized receipts, and any required approval for policy exceptions. Finance may return incomplete reports for correction.
